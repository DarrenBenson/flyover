# Jordan Hale

## Quick Reference

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Cast role** | Negative |
| **Role** | Someone who wants an operational flight-following tool |
| **Context** | Would use a display to dispatch, to alert, or to navigate |

## Who They Are

Jordan wants a picture complete enough to act on: alerts when a flight is late, a trail good enough to vector from, a feed with a service level. That is a different product, sold under different data licences, and it is the use the on-screen disclaimer exists to refuse. Designing for Jordan would pull Flyover into operational claims the feeds do not support.

## End Goals (stated to exclude)

1. Receive an alert when a chosen flight deviates or arrives.
2. Use the track for navigation or for an operational decision.
3. Treat the display as a certified or complete surveillance picture.

## Why we are not designing for them

Serving those goals would require history, alerting, and a warranty about the feed. The position sources evaluated for v1 are hobby or research networks, or commercial contracts Flyover does not hold. A feature that looks operational will be used operationally. The Primary's screen stays a household glance.

## Behaviours & Context

- **Environment:** An ops desk or a cockpit-adjacent decision, not a kitchen.
- **Frequency:** Continuous, with a consequence when the data is wrong.
- **Proficiency:** High, and therefore more likely to trust a confident UI.

## Frustrations

- Hobby tools that look authoritative.
- Missing aircraft presented without a coverage caveat.

## How to handle a request from them

Decline the operational feature. Point at the not-for-navigation requirement. If the request is really "I want a better household detail", it is Samir's and it stays on Ruth's screen. If it needs an alert, a trail archive, or a completeness guarantee, it is out of v1 and needs a new decision, not a quiet addition.
